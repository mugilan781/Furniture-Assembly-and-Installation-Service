TV Wall Mounting

A clean, flush, professionally mounted TV transforms a room. We handle the wall type, cable management, and exact positioning.

From plasterboard with stud-finding to masonry and concrete walls — we know every wall type and the right fixings for each.

All screen sizes from 32" to 100"
Fixed, tilting, and full-motion articulating mounts
Full cable concealment in wall or conduit
Soundbar and device mounting available

Shelf Installation

Floating shelves, alcove shelving, or bracket-mounted systems — perfectly level, perfectly safe, perfectly placed.

We stud-find, use the right rawl plugs, and check weight capacity before installing. Your belongings — and your walls — are in safe hands.

Floating shelves on any wall type
Alcove and chimney breast shelving systems
Bracket systems for heavy-load shelves
Laser levelling for perfect alignment
