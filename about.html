Our Story

From One Man
& a Van, to London's
Most Trusted Team

ASSEMBLIO began in 2014 with a single goal: to prove that furniture installation could be an art form. Founder Thomas Reid, a trained carpenter with a background in bespoke furniture, was tired of watching homeowners receive flat-pack furniture only to struggle with installation or end up with poorly assembled results.

Today, our team of 24 certified installation specialists handles everything from a single IKEA wardrobe to full modular kitchen installations in some of London's most prestigious addresses.

Every project is approached with the same commitment to precision, professionalism, and pride in our work — whether it's a studio flat or a penthouse apartment.
